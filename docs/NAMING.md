# Naming decision

## Working brand: SUTURE

SUTURE describes the product mechanism rather than the technology stack:

- it keeps a policy thread attached across derived positions;
- it closes a broken compliance path after revocation or policy change;
- it supports a distinct visual language of precise joins, seams, and repaired paths;
- it remains broad enough to expand beyond one asset class, chain, or identity provider.

## Brand language

- Primary line: **Compliance that survives composition.**
- Secondary line: **Trace every derived position. Repair every broken policy path.**
- Category: **Compliance continuity and recovery infrastructure for tokenized assets.**

SUTURE remains a working name pending formal trademark and domain review.
